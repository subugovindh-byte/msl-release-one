# The Modernex Stones logo in colour

**Colour around** (option C, chosen), **sawn square**: the block's four sides each one colour — red across the top, green along
the bottom, blue and orange standing between them at the sharp corners — and what is cut in it, the saw's cut and the speckle as in the line logo (ink on
light, light on dark). The wordmark stays solid: ink `#1f1f1f` on light, `#e8e8e8` on dark. Our own shades: red `#D93A2B`,
blue `#1E6FD9`, green `#1F9D55`, orange `#F08C1A`.

| File | Use |
|---|---|
| `modernex-logo-colour-on-light.svg`, `modernex-logo-colour-on-dark.svg` | the logo in colour |
| `modernex-mark-colour-on-light.svg`, `modernex-mark-colour-on-dark.svg` | the mark alone |
| `modernex-favicon-colour.svg` | a small icon on white (no speckle) |
| `colour-sheet.png` | how it looks at each size, on light and dark |

Only where colour belongs — print, signs, slides, the store's own pages, social profiles. The apps keep the one-colour line
logo, which suits their grey screens. The colours go on the frame, never letter by letter across the name.

Drawn by `make-colour.mjs <out-dir> <inter-latin-800-normal.woff>` (it also draws option B). The other options, A (blue
block, colour inside) and B (ink block, colour inside), are in `alternatives/`.
